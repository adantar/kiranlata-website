Our story

Knowing what
you wear matters.

Kiranlata began in New York with a desire for greater honesty in the way sarees are presented. Too often, the distinction between handloom and powerloom is unclear, fiber content is uncertain, and the origin of a saree is left untold.

We believe knowing what you wear matters.

Our collections are chosen with an emphasis on handloom, natural fibers, and the distinct weaving traditions they represent. For us, luxury lies in the human touch, the skill of the artisan, the character of the weave, and the knowledge carried through generations.

Each saree is more than a beautiful textile. It is a piece of living history, made by hand and meant to be worn.

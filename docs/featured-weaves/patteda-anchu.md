# Patteda Anchu featured weave

## Published selection

- One real product: PAT-01, $55, available.
- Product facts: pure cotton, handloom woven, fine black checks on a white/ivory ground, charcoal-grey border with narrow white stripes, no blouse piece.
- Colour roles are recorded separately as `primaryColors` and `accentColors` for the finder. No pallu/anchal colour question is shown because the product record does not separately identify one.
- The product record makes no item-specific GI claim. The Government of India registry records “Patteda Anchu Sarees & Fabrics” as a registered GI category (application 986; validity through 8 September 2032). Category registration is not copied onto PAT-01 as a product claim.
- Homepage masthead uses the single available product and its single photograph, linked to PAT-01. No duplicate tiles or gallery controls are generated.

## Featured-weave structure

1. **The checked cloth** — pure cotton; PAT-01's fine woven checks; its recorded black, ivory and charcoal palette.
2. **The anchu edge** — the defining border band; PAT-01's narrow white border stripes; variation in colors/designs across examples.
3. **A living tradition** — Gajendragad in Gadag district; wedding use documented by craft histories; offering to Goddess Yellamma at Saundatti.

The copy distinguishes broad tradition context from PAT-01-specific details. The colour-variation statement does not claim every historical palette was available in the Kiranlata inventory.

## Research framing and limits

Present Maheshwari as a light silk-cotton handloom tradition associated with Maheshwar, Madhya Pradesh. The Ministry of Textiles and IMPART museum sources describe its silk-cotton fabric, plain/striped/checked body layouts, geometric borders and reversibility. Treat the three feature tabs below as a concise introduction to documented design vocabulary, not as claims that all nine examples appear in Kiranlata's eight current products. Product filters and cards remain based on the inventory records and photographs.

The GI register's registered title is “Maheshwar Sarees & Fabrics.” Use this exact form only where an item-level GI claim is supported by its product record; do not imply that every product is independently GI-certified.

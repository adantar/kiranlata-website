## Research notes

Baluchari is especially associated with silk sarees from Bishnupur, and its woven pallu scenes draw on epic and mythological narratives. The imagery is woven into the textile and framed with ornamental borders. The current Kiranlata selection contains two cotton pieces and one silk piece; the chapter describes the tradition without implying that all three listings share the same fibre or GI status.

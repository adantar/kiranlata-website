## Research and educational taxonomy

1. **Construction**
   - Tope Teni: body and pallu warps joined with interlocking loops.
   - Separate warp sections: body, border, and pallu warps are prepared separately.
   - Kondi / three-shuttle method: Karnataka's handloom department describes three shuttles in the weaving process.
2. **Border**
   - Gomi, also called Ilkal Dadi.
   - Paraspet, including Chikki and Dodda Paras.
   - Gaadi.
3. **Pallu & motif**
   - Red or maroon pallu is a documented traditional form, not a requirement for every Ilkal.
   - Tope Teni geometric bands are associated in the Ministry of Textiles publication with jowar (sorghum) forms.
   - Lattigum is described as a rolling-pin-like motif.

These are heritage examples, not claims that every example appears on every Kiranlata product. Product titles, colors, materials, prices, availability, and image count come from each supplied product record. The current batch's colors should remain product-specific.

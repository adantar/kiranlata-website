## Research notes

- Intellectual Property India lists **Tangail Saree of Bengal**, application 702, as a registered GI for West Bengal. This is the GI name used in public website copy.
- The Government of West Bengal MSME cluster directory records a Fulia Tangail Handloom Cluster producing sarees.
- The Government of West Bengal ODOP brochure describes Tangail sarees as concentrated around Fulia in Nadia district and connects the contemporary cluster to both local weavers and weaving families who migrated from Tangail in present-day Bangladesh. It also identifies colourful geometric designs as a feature of the tradition.

## Educational taxonomy

The feature uses three category-specific dimensions:

1. Cloth — pure cotton, handwoven surface, light structure.
2. Colour & border — doodhe-alta contrast, geometric colour, multicolour edges.
3. Motifs — an illustrative vocabulary supplied for this chapter: Aitihasik / Shonaton geometric forms (Borfi, Karat, Beki, Kathi); Noksha / Rupkatha figure motifs (mythological and religious imagery); and abstract, floral and scenic forms (Megho Dombur, Jhomkolata, Mayur Pekham).

The motif examples are presented as a tradition-wide glossary, not as claims that every motif appears in the current product selection. The three new illustrated icons are bespoke and visually distinct: woven geometry, a figure/trident silhouette, and cloud/creeper/feather forms.

This taxonomy is intentionally different from the Body / Motif / Paar structure used for Dhaniakhali.

## Visual direction

- Palette: Fulia green, doodhe-alta red, warm cotton ivory, mustard and teal accents.
- Background system: a restrained geometric loom-grid pattern rather than Gorod's pale silk field.
- Reusable homepage component: `theme-phulia` in `assets/js/featured-weaves.js` and `assets/css/styles.css`.
- Educational controls use three purpose-drawn, non-repeating symbols: an interlaced loom grid for Cloth, a multicolour framed diamond for Colour & border, and a triad motif mark for Motifs.
- Mobile behavior: stacked intro, collage, and educational reader; same catalogue route and product drawer as desktop.
